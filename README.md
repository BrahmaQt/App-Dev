# My Favorite Series as of the Moment: The Mentalist

## About
**The Mentalist** (2008) is a crime drama series created by **Bruno Heller**. It follows Patrick Jane, a former fake psychic who uses sharp observation to help the California Bureau of Investigation (CBI) solve murders while hunting the serial killer Red John.

## Why I Like It
- Clever cases with *satisfying* twists
- Great mix of humor and suspense
- A cast that works well together
- Iconic scenes, like Jane's staged tricks to draw out a confession

## Main Characters
1. Patrick Jane (Simon Baker)
2. Teresa Lisbon (Robin Tunney)
3. Kimball Cho (Tim Kang)
4. Grace Van Pelt (Amanda Righetti)
5. Wayne Rigsby (Owain Yeoman)

## Favorite Quote
> "I'm not psychic. I'm just very observant."
